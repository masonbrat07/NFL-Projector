Prediction markets

Prices from Kalshi and Polymarket, refreshed by the 8 AM / 5 PM update. A price of 62¢ means the market gives it a 62% chance. For each Kalshi player line, the model's chance is figured at the line closest to 50/50. A gap of 10+ points means the model and the market disagree.

Over/Under performers

This week's projection vs. each player's normal game (recent form before matchup adjustments) for the chosen stat, ranked by percent change. TDs are rushing + receiving touchdowns. Players with a small normal (under 25 rush or receiving yds, 150 pass yds, 0.25 TDs), backups and players ruled out are left out. News & injury report

Player news comes from ESPN's news feed and ESPN's official injury report. Status changes (Out, Doubtful, IR) feed straight into projections: the player is removed and their targets and carries go to teammates. The app updates itself at 8 AM and 5 PM whenever it's open, and catches up on a missed update as soon as you open it.

How accurate is it?

This replays a full season week by week. Each game is predicted using only data from before that week, then scored against what really happened. It covers players averaging 5+ FanDuel points with at least 3 prior games. Lower average miss is better.

Weekly report card

After each week, this scores the projections against what happened. It checks the average miss vs. each player's normal game, whether the Over/Under performer picks came true, and whether the model or the prediction markets were closer. The biggest misses are tagged with the likely cause, and the notes from the weekly review routine are shown with what to change next.

Sync from ESPN

Downloads schedules, betting lines, full box scores, rosters and injury status. The first sync takes about a minute. After that, Quick update only fetches new games.

Claude news feed

A Claude routine reads ESPN and CBS Sports every day at 8 AM and 5 PM, even when this app and Claude are closed. It saves its findings to news-feed.json in a public GitHub repo. Paste that repo's link here, and the app picks the feed up on every update. Items appear in News as Claude · CBS/ESPN. Players Claude rules out are removed, and role changes adjust projections by up to ±30% for 36 hours.

How a projection is built

Final = blend of Recent-form model and Usage model, then × Weather

Usage model (game situation)

ARole

The player's share of the team's targets, carries and pass attempts. It's weighted heavily toward the last few games, so a promotion, a demotion or a depth-chart change shows up quickly. ESPN doesn't publish snap counts; this share is what snaps turn into, and it predicts better.

BDepth chart & injuries

ESPN's depth chart picks the starting QB. When a teammate is ruled out, that player's share of targets and carries is handed out, mostly to players at the same position (80% of it, up to double a player's normal share).

CGame script

Team pass and run volume shifts with the spread: favorites run more, and underdogs throw more. Higher team totals mean more plays.

DEfficiency

Catch rate, yards per target, yards per carry, yards per attempt and TD rates. Each is pulled toward the position average until the player has a real sample, then adjusted for the defense.

EWeather

Kickoff wind, rain or snow, and temperature at the actual stadium (Open-Meteo). Domes are ignored. Wind above 10 mph and precipitation cut passing and push teams toward the run.

The blend is 70% usage for passing stats and 25% usage for receiving and rushing. Those weights tested best on both the 2024 and 2025 seasons. TD% is the chance of at least one rushing or receiving TD, based on the projected TD count.

Recent-form model

1Baseline

A recency-weighted average of the player's last 24 games. For yards and volume, each older game counts 15% less; TDs use a longer memory. Last season counts half. It's then set to 90% of the average to reflect a typical game, because big games pull the plain average up and prop lines sit near the typical outcome.

2Defense

What the opponent allows to that position for that exact stat, compared with the league average. Small samples and rare stats (like QB rushing TDs) are pulled toward average. It's applied at half strength, which tested best.

3Matchup history

How the player has done against this opponent before. It's heavily discounted (2 games ≈ 29% weight) and capped at ±20%, because head-to-head history is mostly noise.

4Vegas

The team's implied points (from ESPN's spread and over/under) compared with its usual scoring. This moves TDs more than yards.

5Home / Away

±1.5%.

Scoring

FanDuel: 0.04 per pass yd, 4 per pass TD, −1 per INT, 0.1 per rush or rec yd, 6 per rush or rec TD, 0.5 per reception, −2 per fumble lost. PrizePicks Fantasy Score is the same except it gives 1 per reception and −1 per fumble lost.

Limitations

True snap counts aren't available from ESPN, so role share stands in for them. Injury boosts depend on ESPN's injury report, and game-day inactives are only known after the latest sync, so run Quick update close to kickoff. Rookies and newly signed players with no NFL games can't be projected until they play. Weather forecasts only go 16 days out. The Accuracy tab shows real performance.
